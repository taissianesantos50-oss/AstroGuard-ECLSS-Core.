The implementation evaluates:

- respiratory rate

The critical state is triggered by the documented synthetic critical rules. The ATTENTION state is triggered when multiple illustrative attention conditions are present.

## 7. Scientific and safety limitation

This implementation is a **research and simulation prototype**.

The physiological values, scenario transitions, and thresholds are synthetic demonstration parameters. They are not clinical limits, diagnostic criteria, biomedical validation, Artemis mission measurements, or flight-qualified safety logic.

This evaluation therefore establishes **software behavior**, not medical validity or spacecraft certification.

## Narration script

### Opening

> When the astronaut is far from Earth, mission awareness must include both the spacecraft environment and the human inside it.

### Architecture

> AstroGuard began with environmental monitoring and evolved into a software architecture that combines environmental telemetry with a deterministic human-health monitoring layer.

### Demonstration

> The simulation starts in a nominal state. As the synthetic telemetry changes, the software transitions to an attention-required state and then to a critical state.

### Interface

> The mission dashboard brings these signals together in a single interface, allowing the operator to observe environmental conditions, human telemetry, actuator states and the current software health state.

### 3D visualization

> The three-dimensional mission view adds spatial and temporal context to the telemetry while preserving the decision engine as a separate software layer.

### Boundary

> This is a research and simulation prototype. Its physiological data are synthetic, its stress indices are visualization metrics, and its thresholds are software-verification parameters rather than clinical or flight-certified limits.

### Closing

> Space exploration is not only about keeping a spacecraft alive. It is about keeping the human inside it healthy enough to continue the mission.

**ASTROGUARD**  
Monitor the environment.  
Understand the human.  
Support the mission.

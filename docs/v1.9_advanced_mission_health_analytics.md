# AstroGuard v1.9 — Advanced Mission Health Analytics

Developer: **Taissiane Thais dos Santos**

Status: **PROPOSED / DEVELOPMENT BRANCH**

## Purpose

v1.9 extends the AstroGuard research/simulation prototype after the stable v1.8 technical package. The goal is to explore deterministic mission-health analytics while preserving the project's low-resource, auditable and reproducible architecture.

## Scope

The v1.9 development track is intended to investigate:

- temporal analysis of environmental and synthetic physiological telemetry;
- deterministic trend indicators;
- persistence and duration of NORMAL / ATTENTION / CRITICAL states;
- correlation of environmental simulation variables with synthetic human-health telemetry;
- mission-health summaries derived from recorded telemetry;
- additional verification scenarios and reproducible test evidence;
- integration of analytical outputs with the existing telemetry, 3D visualization and web dashboard layers.

## Engineering constraints

The implementation should preserve:

1. deterministic behavior where practical;
2. low-resource execution;
3. explicit and inspectable decision logic;
4. reproducible telemetry outputs;
5. separation between implemented, simulated, documented and future capabilities;
6. compatibility with the established v1.4–v1.8 architecture unless a change is explicitly documented.

## Scientific and safety boundary

All physiological telemetry used by this research branch is synthetic unless explicitly documented otherwise. Analytics are software-verification and research outputs, not medical diagnoses or clinical decision-support outputs.

This project does not claim medical-device status, clinical validation, flight qualification, operational spacecraft control, real astronaut telemetry, or NASA/Artemis validation.

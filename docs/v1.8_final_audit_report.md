## 6. Scientific and safety audit

The v1.8 documentation correctly distinguishes the prototype from validated operational systems.

The project does **not** claim:

- clinical validation;
- medical diagnosis;
- medical-device status;
- flight qualification;
- real astronaut telemetry;
- operational spacecraft control;
- NASA/Artemis validation without authoritative evidence.

Human physiological values are explicitly synthetic. Visualization-derived stress representations are described as non-medical metrics.

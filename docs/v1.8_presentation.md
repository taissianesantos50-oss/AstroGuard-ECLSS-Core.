## Scientific boundary

The presentation identifies the project as a **research / simulation prototype**. It does not claim:

- clinical validation;
- flight qualification;
- real astronaut telemetry;
- medical diagnosis;
- operational spacecraft control;
- NASA or Artemis validation without authoritative evidence.

Physiological telemetry is synthetic, and visualization-derived stress indices are non-medical metrics.
